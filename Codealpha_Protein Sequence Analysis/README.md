# Task 1 – DNA/Protein Sequence Analysis

## Objective

To analyze a protein sequence obtained from UniProt and identify homologous sequences using BLAST.

## Protein Selected
- Protein: Hemoglobin subunit beta
- Organism: Homo sapiens
- UniProt ID: P68871
- Gene: HBB
- Sequence type: Protein
- 
## Methodology
- The human hemoglobin beta protein sequence was obtained from UniProt.
- The protein sequence was submitted to BLAST to identify homologous sequences.
- BLAST results were analyzed based on sequence identity, similarity, alignment score, and E-value.
- The results were documented using screenshots and interpreted in the Task 1 report.
