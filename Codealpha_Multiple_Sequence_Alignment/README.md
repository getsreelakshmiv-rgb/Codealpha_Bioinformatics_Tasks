# Task 2 – Multiple Sequence Alignment

## Objective
To perform multiple sequence alignment of hemoglobin beta-chain
proteins from different species and identify conserved regions
and important residues.

## Tool Used
Clustal Omega 1.2.4

## Protein
Hemoglobin beta-chain (HBB)

## Species
- Human – P68871
- Chimpanzee – P68873
- Gorilla – XP_018891709.1
- Mouse – P02088
- Horse – F6RDD3

## Results
The alignment showed high conservation among the selected
hemoglobin beta-chain proteins. Conserved regions and sequence
variations were identified from the alignment.
